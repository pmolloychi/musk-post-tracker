This site counts every post Elon Musk made on X in 2026, as logged by an automated tracker that checks his account every hour. The tracker's code is public.

**Topics.** For each post, Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, picks one or two topics from a fixed list of 19. A post counts as political if any of its topics is one of these 12: Government/DOGE, US politics & elections, Immigration, Race & ethnicity, DEI policy, Religion, LGBTQ issues, Trans issues, Women & gender, Media & journalists, Free speech/censorship and Foreign politics. Nobody reviews the topics, and some will be wrong.

**Retweets.** Retweets count, because choosing to share a post with his followers is a choice. Every chart shows what he wrote apart from what he retweeted.

**Flags and the random sample.** Claude also suggests whether a post may be bigoted, and in what way. Those suggestions are private and never shown here, except on posts Parker has reviewed. To measure how often the suggestions hold up, 100 flagged posts were picked at random using a public random number (a NIST Randomness Beacon value) from a minute chosen and published in advance, and Parker reviewed each one. A first draw on Nov. 3 was thrown out because its lock wasn't published before review began. The rate counts any post Parker upheld. A stricter count, where Parker's judgment of the harm matches Claude's, is shown with it.

**How the review form works.** When Parker reviews a post, the form starts with Claude's suggested harm type, a guess at what Musk did (wrote it, quoted it, retweeted it) and Claude's one-sentence reason for the flag. Parker accepts or changes each of them. The "Why" line on a reviewed post may be Claude's draft, kept because Parker agreed with it.

**What the lock proves.** The pool's fingerprint and the beacon minute were public before the random number existed, so nobody could pick or redraw the sample. It can't show that the pool it was drawn from held every flagged post, because the flags themselves stay private.

**Selected examples** are other posts Parker reviewed and upheld. They are not part of the rate.
